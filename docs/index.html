About Me

I am a journalism and media studies doctoral student at the School of Communication and Information, Rutgers University. I'm fascinated by the feedback loop between screens and real life. Hence, my research focuses on the human side of media—how we use media to make sense of complex events and information, negotiate identity, power, and belonging, and push for social change across different political and cultural settings.
